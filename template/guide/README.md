# The guide: use AI well, check it, stay safe

**Cut the slop. Know your AI. Let it know you.**

> Each lesson is one habit for the three things slop skips: deciding, understanding and checking.
> Your AI can help with all three. It can't do them for you.

Short lessons, each with one idea, one example and one thing to try. Reading them costs nothing: they're
plain files, and your AI doesn't need to open them.

1. **[Working with an AI](basics.md):** what you're talking to, giving it context, asking for options,
   explaining back, resetting when it goes in circles, and your usage as a budget.
2. **[Checking what it gives you](checking.md):** why confident isn't correct, which claims to check first,
   how to check each kind, and when "good enough" is enough. It starts where the interview's scenes left off.
3. **[Staying safe](safety.md):** what not to paste, keys and `.env`, before anything goes live, letting AI
   act for you, your data, learning honestly, and a thinking partner, not a friend.
4. **[The ten slips](ai-patterns.md):** what can go wrong when work goes unchecked, the names your companion
   uses when it slips, and how to catch each one yourself.

For reference:

- **[Usage](usage.md):** where each tool shows how much is left, and what fits in it.
- **[Models](models.md):** which model and effort to use for what, how to switch, and which of your tool's own
  modes fit your companion.
